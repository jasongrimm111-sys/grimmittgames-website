Cookie policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

The short version: grimmittgames.com does not use cookies or any similar technology, so there is nothing to accept or turn off.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website can save on your device, for example to remember settings or to track visits. Similar technologies include browser storage (such as "local storage"), tracking pixels and device fingerprinting. Under the UK's Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), a website must ask your permission before using any of these unless they are strictly necessary.

Our web host, GitHub Pages, does not set cookies on the website. If you follow a link to another site, such as the App Store, that site's own cookie policy applies.

Google Analytics

If you accept, the site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. It sets these cookies:

  • _ga: tells one visitor from another (kept for 2 years)
  • _ga_JW23JSV7Q9: keeps track of your current visit (kept for 2 years)

Google Analytics records things like the pages you view, your type of device and browser, and roughly where you are (worked out from your IP address, which isn't stored). We don't use it to identify you, for advertising, or to follow you on other sites. If you reject, or don't choose, these cookies aren't set and Google Analytics isn't loaded.

Your choice

You can change your choice at any time here, or with Cookie settings at the bottom of any page.

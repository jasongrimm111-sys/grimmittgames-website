The studio

Small studio. Big on craft.

Grimmitt Games Limited is an independent games developer and publisher based in Bedford, England. We design, build and publish our own games, and care about every frame of them.

Fair by design

No pay-to-win. Everything can be earned by playing, purchases are always optional, and adverts only ever play when you choose them.

Made for everyone

Games the whole family can enjoy: no accounts, no chat and no tracking, playable offline and in 16 languages.

Polished to the last frame

One-touch controls that feel right, lively worlds, and a perfect run of every level to show you how it's done.
